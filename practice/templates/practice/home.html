UGC NET Paper 1 Units Covered

The NTA UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus consists of 10 units that test general aptitude for teaching and research. Our PYQ collection covers: Teaching Aptitude, Research Aptitude, Comprehension, Communication, Mathematical Reasoning and Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Data Interpretation, Information and Communication Technology (ICT), People, Development and Environment, and Higher Education System. Each unit is organised into PYQ sets of up to 10 questions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is UGC NET Paper 1?

UGC NET Paper 1 is the general aptitude paper conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) and Assistant Professor eligibility. It covers teaching and research aptitude, reasoning, communication, ICT and other general aptitude topics.

Are these UGC NET PYQ sets free?

Yes, all PYQ sets on this platform are completely free. No registration, login or payment is required. You can practise subject-wise questions with instant answers and explanations anytime.

How many questions are available?

There are currently {{ total_site_questions }} UGC NET Paper 1 PYQs across all 10 subjects. Exam year, date or shift is shown when that information is available for a question.

Does this cover NTA NET JRF as well?

Yes. Paper 1 is common for both UGC NET (Assistant Professor eligibility) and JRF (Junior Research Fellowship). The same syllabus and question pattern apply to both, so these PYQ sets are relevant for NTA NET JRF preparation.

How do the PYQ sets work?

Choose a subject, then select a PYQ set. Questions are shown one at a time with four options (A, B, C, D). Select your answer to see instant feedback — correct or wrong — along with a detailed explanation. Use Previous and Next to move between questions, or Submit at any time to see your score and accuracy. You can retry the set.
