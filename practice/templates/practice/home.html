Frequently Asked Questions

What is UGC NET Paper 1?

UGC NET Paper 1 is the general aptitude paper conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for the eligibility of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) and Assistant Professor positions in Indian universities. It covers 10 units including Teaching Aptitude, Research Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Data Interpretation, ICT and more. The exam has 50 MCQs to be answered in 60 minutes.

Is this UGC NET mock test free?

Yes, all practice tests on this platform are completely free. No registration, login or payment is required. You can practise subject-wise questions with instant answers and explanations anytime.

How many questions are available?

There are currently {{ total_site_questions }}+ questions available across all 10 subjects of UGC NET Paper 1. Questions include both original practice MCQs and Previous Year Questions (PYQs) with exam dates and shifts. New questions are added regularly.

Does this cover NTA NET JRF as well?

Yes. Paper 1 is common for both UGC NET (Assistant Professor eligibility) and JRF (Junior Research Fellowship). The same syllabus and question pattern apply to both, so all practice sets here are relevant for NTA NET JRF preparation.

How does the practice test work?

Choose a subject, then select a practice set. Questions are shown one at a time with four options (A, B, C, D). Select your answer to see instant feedback — correct or wrong — along with a detailed explanation. You can skip or go back to any question. After finishing, you see your total score, accuracy percentage and can retry the set.
